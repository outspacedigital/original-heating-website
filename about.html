Our History & Expertise

15+ Years of Dedicated Heating Services in Northern Ireland

Founded with a commitment to honesty, technical excellence, and rapid customer service, Original Heating Limited has grown into one of Northern Ireland’s most respected heating companies. Operating out of Derry / Londonderry and servicing Belfast, Lisburn, Antrim, Down, Tyrone, and Fermanagh, we take immense pride in keeping local families and businesses warm.

Whether installing high-efficiency A-rated combi boilers, diagnosing complex emergency faults, or maintaining commercial heating plants, our Gas Safe certified engineers bring decades of combined master craftsmanship to every project.
