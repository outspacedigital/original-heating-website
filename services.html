Service #4

Annual Boiler Servicing & Landlord CP12

Annual servicing is required to maintain your manufacturer's warranty, prevent dangerous carbon monoxide leaks, and maximize fuel efficiency.

Landlord Gas Safety Certificates (CP12)

By law, landlords in Northern Ireland must obtain an annual gas safety certificate for every gas appliance in their rental properties. We provide fast digital CP12 reporting.

Our 20-Point Service Inspection Checklist

  • Flue gas analyzer safety combustion test
  • Burner & main heat exchanger clean and inspection
  • Flame failure safety device operation check
  • Gas pressure & burner rate verification
  • Magnetic system filter cleaning
  • Official Gas Safe Service Record issuance
